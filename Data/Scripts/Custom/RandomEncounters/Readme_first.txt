1. Unzip to Scripts directory. I recommend creating a new dir under Scripts called Custom and unzipping it there so it'll end up like this: Scripts\Custom\RandomEncounters
2. Copy/Cut/Move RandomEncounters.xml to Data dir.
3. Have a look at RandomEncounters.xml and customize it to your own liking.
